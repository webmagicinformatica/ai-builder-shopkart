# ShopKart policy sheet (current policies, November 2026)

## Returns
- Most products: return within **14 days of delivery** (counted from the delivery date, not the order date). Unused, original packaging, all tags, accessories and free gifts.
- Footwear: **10 days** from delivery, unworn outdoors, in the original box. (It was 7 days before 1 June 2026. Answers that say 7 days for footwear, or 30 days for anything, are out of date.)
- Electronics (phones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, headphones, earbuds, chargers, cables, power banks): **7 days** from delivery, **unopened** only, unless the item is faulty or damaged. Opened and working: not returnable, use the manufacturer's warranty.
- Opened headphones and earbuds: returnable **only if faulty** (no sound, one side only, case not charging, Bluetooth not pairing after a reset), for hygiene reasons. Fit or comfort is not a fault.
- Laptop bags, phone cases and covers are accessories: 14 days, if unused.
- Beauty: 7 days, unopened only. Grocery and gift cards: not returnable. Innerwear, swimwear, personalised items: not returnable.
- To return: My Orders → choose the item → Return. Pickup is usually the next working day.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
- Report within the category's window (electronics 7 days, footwear 10 days, most others 14 days) with a photo of the damage and of the shipping label, in My Orders → Report a problem. The refunds team replies within 48 hours.
- A cracked screen on arrival is a damaged item (return or replacement within 7 days), not a warranty claim. Damage after delivery (dropped, liquid, cracked screen) is **not covered** by return or warranty.

## Sale and clearance
- Sale items follow the normal window, but get **no price-drop refund**.
- Price-drop refund (non-sale items only): the difference, if the price drops within 7 days of delivery, once per order.
- Clearance or Final sale items: no return for change of mind. A clearance item with a **defect** (for example a split sole) can be **exchanged** within the window; a refund only if no replacement is available.

## Refunds
- Refunds go to the **original payment method** within **5–7 working days** of pickup (UPI, card and net banking). Gift card payments: back to the gift card within 24 hours.
- EMI: a full return cancels the EMI plan. A partial return (for example, the bag from a laptop and bag combo) refunds that item's share of the price as a lump sum to the card; the EMI for the rest continues.
- Cash on delivery (COD): the customer adds a bank account (account number and IFSC) in My Orders → Refund details; the refund is sent by NEFT within 5–7 working days after the bank details are verified, or as a gift card balance within 24 hours.
- Delivery fees are refunded only when the item was damaged, defective or wrong.
- **Any refund above ₹5,000 needs a human** (a support lead approves it within 1 working day). The Copilot may raise the request, but must not approve or promise it.
- Refund not received after **7 working days** from the refund SMS: raise a ticket in Help → Refunds with the **UPI reference number** (UTR) or the card refund reference (ARN). Ten calendar days usually means seven working days.

## Orders, cancellations and address changes
- Cancel in My Orders → Cancel until the status is **Shipped**. Free. After shipping it cannot be cancelled: refuse it at the door, or accept and return it.
- Cancelled prepaid order: refund in 5–7 working days. COD: nothing was paid, nothing to refund.
- Change the address once, **before dispatch**, in My Orders → Change address. After dispatch the Copilot must hand over to a human.
- Tracking: every order gets a tracking number at dispatch; track it in **My Orders** or with the **SMS link**. The first scan can take 24 hours.

## Delivery
- Delivery charge: order value ₹499 and above is **free in metro cities** and **₹49 in other cities**; below ₹499 it is ₹49 (metro) or ₹79 (other cities); ₹2,999 and above is free everywhere. Metro cities: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad. Kochi, Jaipur, Lucknow and others are "other cities" (a ₹799 order to Kochi pays ₹49).
- Standard delivery: 2–4 working days in metro cities, 4–7 elsewhere. Express ₹99 extra.
- Deliveries happen **between 9 am and 9 pm, Monday to Saturday**.
- Parcel marked delivered but not received: report within **48 hours**. Claims after 48 hours, or for orders above ₹5,000, go to a human.

## Payments
- Cash on delivery: orders up to **₹10,000**, with a ₹30 COD fee.
- Card EMI: orders of **₹3,000 and above**, 3, 6, 9 or 12 months.
- Charged twice: the extra amount is reversed automatically in 5–7 working days; after 7 working days, raise a ticket with the UPI reference number.
- Gift cards are valid for **1 year** (from issue, activation or credit).
- Never ask for a card number, CVV, OTP or UPI PIN.

## Support and handover
- Support hours: **9 am to 9 pm IST, Monday to Saturday**. Toll-free: **1800-120-7575**.
- Hand over to a human for: refunds above ₹5,000, requests outside policy, questions these rules don't answer, legal threats, safety issues, privacy incidents, payment disputes beyond the standard timelines, address changes after dispatch, and customers who ask for a person twice. A human replies within one working day (4 working hours for refunds above ₹5,000).
