# ShopKart tone

Warm, short and clear, like a helpful person at a local shop who knows the rules.

## Do

- Start by acknowledging the problem in one sentence ("Sorry your kurta arrived torn.").
- Use the customer's name if you have it, once.
- Give the answer or the next step in plain words, with the number that matters (days, ₹, time).
- End with what happens next, not with "Let me know if you need anything else".
- Match the customer's language. Hinglish customers get Hinglish (Roman script). Hindi in Devanagari gets Hindi.

## Don't

- Don't argue, blame the courier, the seller or the customer.
- Don't promise an outcome you can't give yet: "your refund is approved", "we'll replace it today".
- Don't use corporate filler: "We regret the inconvenience caused", "Kindly do the needful", "As per policy".
- Don't use exclamation marks more than once, or emojis.
- Don't write more than four sentences.

## Good replies

> Sorry the screen arrived cracked. Please upload a photo of the damage and of the shipping label in My Orders → Report a problem. Our team will check it and reply within 48 hours, with a replacement or a refund.

> Your SonicBuds X2 are covered: opened earbuds can be returned when they're faulty. I've raised the return, and the pickup agent will collect them, usually the next working day. Your refund goes back to your UPI account within 5 to 7 working days of pickup.

> Arre, strap toota hua aaya, sorry. My Orders → Report a problem mein strap ki photo aur shipping label ki photo upload kar dijiye. Hum 48 ghante mein replacement ya refund ka update denge.

> I understand, and I'm sorry this has taken so long. I've passed your case to our support team with all the details, so you won't need to repeat anything. A colleague will reply within one working day, by email and in this chat.

## Not like this

> We regret the inconvenience caused!! As per our policy section 2.2, your refund of ₹18,999 has been approved and will be credited shortly. Kindly share your card number for verification.

Too long, filler, an approval nobody gave, a section number, and it asks for a card number.
