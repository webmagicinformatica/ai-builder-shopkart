# Reply templates

Fill in the parts in [brackets]. Keep the reply to four sentences. Translate to Hindi or Hinglish when the customer writes that way.

## Return, inside the window
Yes, you can return your [product]: it's within the [N]-day return window for [category]. Open My Orders, choose the item and tap Return, and a pickup is usually booked for the next working day. Your refund goes back to your [payment method] within 5 to 7 working days of pickup.

## Return, outside the window (hand over)
Your [product] was delivered [N] days ago, so it's outside our [N]-day return window for [category]. I can't change the window myself, but I've passed your request to our support team for review. A colleague will reply within one working day.

## Damaged or defective on arrival
Sorry your [product] arrived [damaged / not working]. Please upload a photo of the [damage] and of the shipping label in My Orders → Report a problem. Our team will reply within 48 hours with a free replacement, or a refund if you prefer.

## Damaged, refund above ₹5,000 (hand over)
Sorry your [product] arrived [damaged]. Please upload a photo of the damage and of the shipping label in My Orders → Report a problem. Because the amount is above ₹5,000, a support lead reviews the refund, and you'll hear from them within 4 working hours.

## Not returnable
I'm sorry, [product / opened earbuds / clearance items] can't be returned [for change of mind / once opened]. [If it develops a fault, you can report it in My Orders → Report a problem.] [Offer the next best thing: a size exchange, the warranty, or a handover if they want an exception.]

## Refund status
Your refund for [order] was started on [date] and goes back to your [payment method] within 5 to 7 working days of pickup. If it hasn't arrived by [date], raise a ticket in Help → Refunds with the [UTR / ARN] number from the SMS, and the bank can trace it.

## Ask for the order ID
Happy to check that for you. Could you share your order number? It starts with SK, like SK48213, and it's in your confirmation email and in My Orders.

## Hand over (any reason)
I understand, and I've passed this to our support team with all the details, so you won't need to explain it again. A colleague will reply [within one working day / within 4 working hours / within 2 hours], by email and in this chat.

## Hinglish starters
- "Sorry, aapka [product] [damaged] aaya." / "Haan, aap [product] return kar sakte hain."
- "Maine aapki request support team ko bhej di hai, ek colleague [time] mein reply karega."
