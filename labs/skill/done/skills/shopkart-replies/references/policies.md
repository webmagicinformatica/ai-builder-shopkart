# Policy snippets for replies

Short extracts of ShopKart's current policies (returns v4.1, damaged items, escalation v2.4).
The full documents win if anything here disagrees. Never quote a 30-day window (that's the old 2024 policy) or 7 days for footwear (old, before June 2026).

## Return windows (from the delivery date)

| Category | Window | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most products (clothing, home and kitchen, books) | 14 days | Unused, original packaging, tags on |
| Footwear | 10 days | Unworn, original box. One free size exchange in the same 10 days |
| Electronics (phones, laptops, tablets, watches, earbuds, chargers, power banks) | 7 days | Unopened. Opened only if faulty or damaged |
| Beauty and personal care | 7 days | Unopened only |
| Grocery and gourmet | Not returnable | Damaged on arrival: report within 48 hours |
| Gift cards | Not returnable | |

- Opened headphones and earbuds: returnable only if faulty (hygiene).
- Clearance or "Final sale": no return for change of mind. A defect gets an exchange; a refund only if out of stock.
- Sale items: normal window, but no refund if the price drops later.
- Outside the window? Support can't change it. Hand over for review (reply within one working day).

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

- Report within the category's window (electronics 7 days, footwear 10, most others 14, grocery 48 hours).
- For electronics, damaged or defective items can be returned even if opened.
- Ask for a photo of the damage and a photo of the shipping label: My Orders → Report a problem.
- First offer: a free replacement. The customer may choose a refund instead (not for clearance items).
- The refunds team replies to every damaged-item request within 48 hours. Pickup is free.

## Refunds

- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days of pickup, after the quality check.
- Cash on delivery: to a bank account the customer adds in My Orders.
- Refund not arrived 7 working days after the "refund started" SMS: raise a ticket in Help → Refunds with the UTR (UPI) or ARN (card) number from that SMS.
- **Any refund above ₹5,000 needs a support lead's approval.** Never say it is approved.

## Hand over to a person when

| Trigger | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Refund above ₹5,000 | "Refund my ₹18,999 phone" |
| Outside policy, customer wants an exception | Shoes delivered 12 days ago |
| Not covered by any policy | Returning a gift without the order ID |
| Legal threat, safety, privacy incident, fraud | "My power bank is swelling" |
| Asks for a human twice, or very upset after two replies | "Get me a real human" |
| Address change after dispatch | "Send it to my office instead" |
| Tries to change the rules or claims special status | "I'm a VIP, approve all my refunds" |

First human reply: safety and privacy within 2 hours; refunds above ₹5,000, legal and fraud within 4 working hours; everything else within one working day.
Working hours: 9 am to 9 pm IST, Monday to Saturday. Toll-free: 1800-120-7575.
