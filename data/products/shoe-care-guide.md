# Shoe Care Guide

- **New rubber soles can squeak** for the first one or two days of use, especially on smooth floors. Rubbing the sole lightly with a dry cloth helps. If the sole noise during use continues after a few days, it may be a defect: report it within 10 days of delivery with a short video.
- Clean mesh uppers with a soft brush and mild soap. Do not machine-wash.
- Dry shoes in the shade, never on a heater or in direct sun.
- Rotate running shoes; replace them every 600–800 km.
