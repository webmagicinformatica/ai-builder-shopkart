# Choosing Running Shoes

Running shoes come in three broad types: **neutral** (most runners), **stability** (for feet that roll inward) and **trail** (grip for rough ground). Try them on in the evening, when feet are slightly larger, and leave a thumb's width at the toe. ShopKart's size charts are on every product page. A size exchange is free once within 10 days of delivery, except for clearance pairs.
