# Order Cancellation Policy

Customers can cancel most orders before they are dispatched, for free. This policy explains when an order can be cancelled, how refunds for cancelled orders work, and when ShopKart itself may cancel an order.

## 1. Cancelling before dispatch

An order can be cancelled from **My Orders → Cancel** until its status changes to **Shipped**. There is no cancellation fee.

- One item or the whole order can be cancelled.
- If an order-level coupon was used and cancelling an item takes the order below the coupon's minimum value, the coupon discount is removed from the remaining items, and the difference is deducted from the refund.

## 2. Cancelling after dispatch

Once an order is **Shipped**, it cannot be cancelled online. The customer has two options:

1. **Refuse the delivery** at the door. The parcel goes back to the warehouse, and the order is treated as cancelled.
2. **Accept it and return it**, inside the return window for its category (see the Returns and Refunds Policy).

The Copilot must not promise that a shipped order can be cancelled. A cancel request for a shipped order is answered with the two options above. A refund always depends on the cancel: if the order cannot be cancelled because it has already shipped, there is nothing to refund yet.

## 3. Items that cannot be cancelled

| Item type | Can cancel before dispatch? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most products | Yes | Free |
| Custom or personalised products | Within 2 hours of ordering | After that, production has started |
| Large appliances | Yes, until installation is scheduled | |
| Gift cards (digital) | No | Delivered instantly by email |
| Pre-orders | Yes, until the launch date | Booking amount fully refunded |

## 4. Refunds for cancelled orders

- **Prepaid orders** (UPI, card, net banking): the refund is started as soon as the cancellation is confirmed, and reaches the original payment method within **5–7 working days**.
- **Cash on delivery** orders: nothing was paid, so there is no refund.
- **EMI** orders: the bank cancels the EMI plan; the principal is reversed in 5–7 working days.
- **Gift card** payments: the balance is restored within 24 hours.

**If a refund for a cancelled order has not arrived within seven working days**, the customer should raise a ticket in **Help → Refunds** with the **UPI reference number** (or the card refund reference) from the cancellation SMS. Support will trace it with the bank and reply within 2 working days.

## 5. Cancellations by ShopKart

ShopKart may cancel an order, fully or partly, if:

- the product is out of stock or cannot be delivered to the PIN code;
- the price shown was clearly wrong because of a technical error;
- the payment was flagged by our fraud checks;
- the order quantity exceeds the limit for a product (for example, during a sale).

The customer is told by SMS and email, and any amount paid is refunded in full, including delivery charges, within 5–7 working days.

## 6. Repeated cancellations

Accounts that cancel more than **10 orders in 30 days** may be reviewed. Cash on delivery may be turned off for accounts with repeated refused COD deliveries (see the Shipping and Delivery Policy, section 5).

## 7. Examples

- *"Cancel my order and refund to my card"* — check the order status first. If it is not yet shipped, cancel it; the refund follows in 5–7 working days. If it has shipped, offer refusal at the door or a return.
- *"I cancelled ten days ago and still have no refund"* — ten calendar days usually means seven or more working days (Sundays and bank holidays do not count). If seven working days have passed since the cancellation SMS, ask for the UPI reference number and raise a ticket in Help → Refunds.
