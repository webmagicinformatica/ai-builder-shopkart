# ShopKart Gift Card Policy

ShopKart Gift Cards are prepaid cards that can be used to pay for orders on ShopKart. They are issued under the RBI rules for closed-system prepaid payment instruments. This policy covers buying, using, and the limits on gift cards. It does not cover returning products that were received as gifts.

## 1. Types of gift card

| Type | Values | Delivery | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E-gift card | ₹100 to ₹10,000, any amount | By email or SMS, within 15 minutes | 1 year from issue |
| Physical gift card | ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹5,000 | Shipped like an order | 1 year from activation |
| Refund gift card balance | Any amount | Credited to the account | 1 year from credit |
| Corporate gift cards | ₹500 to ₹10,000 per card, bulk | By email | 1 year from issue |

The validity is printed on the card and shown in **Account → Gift Cards**. Every time a balance is used, the remaining balance keeps its original expiry date; using it does not extend the validity.

## 2. Buying gift cards

- Gift cards can be paid for with UPI, cards or net banking. They **cannot be bought with cash on delivery, EMI, or another gift card**.
- A customer can buy up to **₹50,000** of gift cards per month. Corporate orders above this need a business account.
- Coupons and sale discounts do not apply to gift card purchases.

## 3. Using gift cards

- Add the card in **Account → Gift Cards** with its 16-digit number and 6-digit PIN. The balance is then used at checkout.
- A gift card can be **combined with one other payment method** (UPI, card or net banking) when the balance is not enough. It cannot be combined with cash on delivery.
- Up to **5 gift cards** can be added to an account.
- Gift card balance cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

## 4. Refunds and cancellations

- Gift cards are **not refundable** and cannot be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it.
- E-gift cards cannot be cancelled once delivered.
- A physical gift card order can be cancelled before dispatch; after delivery it cannot be returned.
- When an order paid with a gift card is cancelled or returned, the gift card part goes back to the gift card balance within 24 hours, with the card's original expiry date. If the card has already expired, ShopKart issues a new balance valid for 90 days.

## 5. Lost or stolen cards

ShopKart cannot replace a lost or stolen gift card that has already been used. If a card is reported before it is used, with proof of purchase (order ID or payment receipt), the balance is blocked and moved to a new card, once.

Never share a gift card number and PIN with anyone. ShopKart will never ask a customer to pay a fee, tax or "unlocking charge" in gift cards. **Requests to pay with gift cards over a phone call are almost always a scam.**

## 6. Expired cards

Expired balances cannot be used. A customer can request a one-time extension of **up to 90 days** within 30 days after expiry; this needs a human agent's approval. After that, the balance is forfeited as per the card's terms.

## 7. Balance and statement

The balance and the last 20 transactions are shown in **Account → Gift Cards**. Customers can also check a balance without logging in at shopkart.in/giftcard-balance, using the card number and PIN.

## 8. Examples

- *"Can I pay ₹6,000 using my ₹4,000 gift card and UPI?"* Yes. The gift card covers ₹4,000 and UPI covers ₹2,000.
- *"Can I use COD with my gift card?"* No. Gift cards can only be combined with UPI, card or net banking.
- *"Can I get cash for my gift card?"* No. Balances are not refundable.
