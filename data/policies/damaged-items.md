# Damaged, Defective and Wrong Items Policy

Sometimes a product arrives broken, does not work, or is not what was ordered. This policy explains how customers report it, what proof is needed, and what ShopKart offers.

## 1. Definitions

- **Damaged:** the product or its packaging arrived physically broken, cracked, dented, torn or leaking. Example: a phone or smartwatch with a cracked screen on arrival, a torn kurta.
- **Defective:** the product looks fine but does not work as it should. Example: earbuds with no sound in one ear, a mixer that does not start.
- **Wrong item:** a different product, size, colour or quantity than the one ordered, or another customer's item.

## 2. Time limits for reporting

Report the problem **within the return window for the product's category**, counted from the delivery date:

| Category | Report damaged or defective within | Report wrong item within |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electronics | 7 days | 7 days |
| Footwear | 10 days | 10 days |
| Clothing, home, books and most others | 14 days | 14 days |
| Grocery and gourmet | 48 hours | 48 hours |
| Large appliances | 48 hours (at installation) | 48 hours |

For electronics, damaged and defective items can be returned **even if the box has been opened**. This is the exception to the "unopened only" rule for electronics.

Reports after these limits are not accepted automatically. They go to a human agent, who may offer a warranty claim instead (see the Warranty Policy).

## 3. Proof needed

Ask the customer for:

1. A clear **photo of the damage** (or a short video for a defect).
2. A photo of the **shipping label** on the outer box.
3. For wrong items: a photo of the product received, showing its label or tag.

Photos can be uploaded in **My Orders → Report a problem**, or sent in the chat. For phones, laptops and tablets above ₹10,000, a **video of the unboxing** helps if the damage is disputed, but it is not required.

## 4. What ShopKart offers

| Situation | First option | If not possible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Damaged on arrival | Free replacement | Full refund |
| Defective on arrival (dead on arrival) | Free replacement | Full refund |
| Wrong item | Correct item sent; wrong item picked up | Full refund |
| Clearance / final sale item, damaged or defective | Exchange for the same product | Refund only if out of stock |

The customer can choose a refund instead of a replacement for damaged or wrong items, except for clearance items. Refunds for damaged, defective and wrong items include the delivery charge and the COD fee.

Refunds follow the Refund Processing Policy. **A refund above ₹5,000 needs approval from a support lead**, even when the damage is clear. The refunds team replies to every damaged-item request **within 48 hours**.

## 5. Pickup

ShopKart picks up the damaged or wrong item for free. The customer should keep it with all accessories and packaging. If the item is a hazard (a leaking power bank, broken glass), the customer should not use it and should tell support; the pickup is then booked with special packaging.

## 6. Another customer's item or invoice

If a parcel contains another customer's product, invoice or personal details, treat it as a **privacy incident**:

- Do not ask the customer to read out or share the other person's details.
- Hand the case to a human agent at once (see the Escalation and Human Handover Policy). The privacy team is informed.
- The customer is asked to keep the documents aside; a pickup is arranged.

## 7. What the Copilot may do

- Explain this policy, collect the photos and the order ID, and raise a replacement or refund request.
- It must not say a refund has been processed. A good reply: "I'm sorry your order arrived damaged. I can't issue refunds myself, but I can raise a request now, and the team usually replies within 48 hours. Could you share a photo of the damage?"
