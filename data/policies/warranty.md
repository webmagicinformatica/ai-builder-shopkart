# Warranty Policy

Most products sold on ShopKart carry the **manufacturer's warranty**. ShopKart does not repair products itself, but we help customers start a claim and, in some cases, replace a product directly. This policy explains warranty periods, what is needed to claim, and what is not covered.

## 1. Warranty periods

| Product type | Warranty | Who handles the claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile phones | 1 year | Brand service centre |
| Phone batteries and in-box chargers | 6 months | Brand service centre |
| Laptops and tablets | 1 year | Brand service centre |
| Smartwatches and fitness bands | 1 year | Brand service centre |
| Headphones and earbuds | 1 year | Brand service centre |
| Power banks, cables, chargers (sold separately) | 6 months | ShopKart (replacement) |
| Large appliances | 1 year (compressor/motor: up to 10 years, see brand terms) | Brand, at home |
| Small kitchen appliances (mixers, kettles) | 1–2 years, see product page | Brand service centre |
| ShopKart Basics products | 1 year | ShopKart (replacement) |

The warranty starts on the **delivery date**, not the order date.

## 2. Phone warranty claims

Claims for mobile phones need the **original invoice**. Download it from **My Orders → Invoice** if it was lost; a ShopKart invoice downloaded this way is accepted by all brand service centres.

To claim:

1. Back up your data. Service centres may reset the phone.
2. Find the nearest authorised service centre on the brand's website.
3. Take the phone, the invoice and a photo ID. The box is not required.
4. The service centre diagnoses the fault and repairs or replaces the phone, according to the brand's policy.

Warranty is valid only in India. Phones bought on ShopKart cannot be claimed under warranty abroad.

## 3. Dead on arrival (DOA)

If an electronic product does not work at all when it arrives, the customer can report it **within 7 days of delivery**. This is handled as a return, not a warranty claim (see the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 2.2). ShopKart arranges a pickup and sends a replacement, or refunds if no replacement is available.

For phones and laptops, some brands require a **DOA certificate** from their service centre before the replacement. The product page says when this applies.

## 4. ShopKart replacement warranty

For accessories sold separately (power banks, cables, chargers) and for **ShopKart Basics** products, ShopKart handles the claim itself:

- Raise a claim in **My Orders → Warranty claim** with a short video or photo of the fault.
- If approved, a replacement is sent; the faulty product is picked up at the same time.
- If the product is no longer sold, a refund of the price paid is given as a ShopKart Gift Card balance.

## 5. What warranty does not cover

- Physical damage, liquid damage, or a cracked screen caused after delivery
- Damage from unauthorised repair or software modification (rooting, jailbreaking)
- Normal wear: scratches, battery capacity loss below 80% after 1 year
- Consumables: ear tips, straps, filters, blades
- Products with a removed or tampered serial number

A cracked screen **on arrival** is not a warranty claim. It is a damaged item, covered by the Damaged, Defective and Wrong Items Policy within the return window.

## 6. Extended warranty

ShopKart Protect plans (1 or 2 extra years) can be bought within **30 days of delivery** for phones, laptops and large appliances. They are provided by our insurance partner and have their own terms. Cancelling within 15 days of purchase gives a full refund of the plan price.

## 7. When to involve a human

Warranty disputes with a brand, repeated failures (three repairs on the same fault), and claims for products above ₹50,000 can be escalated to ShopKart's Electronics Care team. See the Escalation and Human Handover Policy.
