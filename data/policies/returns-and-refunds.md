# Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy explains how customers can return products bought on ShopKart and how refunds are paid. It applies to all orders delivered in India on or after 1 June 2026. Category rules in section 2.2 override the general window in section 2.1.

## 1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers products sold and shipped by ShopKart. Products from marketplace sellers show "Sold by" with the seller's name on the product page; most follow this policy, but a seller may set a shorter window, and the product page always shows the window that applies.

## 2. Returns

### 2.1 Return window

You can return most products within **14 days of delivery**. The 14 days start on the delivery date shown in your order details, not on the order date. The product must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags, accessories, manuals and free gifts that came with it.

To start a return, open **My Orders**, choose the item and tap **Return**. A pickup is usually booked for the next working day. Keep the product packed and ready.

### 2.2 Category return windows

Some categories have their own window. When a category is listed here, its window replaces the 14 days in section 2.1.

| Category | Return window | Condition | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clothing and accessories | 14 days | Unworn, tags attached | Innerwear and swimwear not returnable |
| Footwear | 10 days | Unworn, original box | See the Footwear Policy for size exchanges |
| Electronics | 7 days | Unopened, seal intact | Opened items only if faulty or damaged |
| Home and kitchen | 14 days | Unused | Large appliances: see section 2.5 |
| Books | 14 days | No markings | |
| Beauty and personal care | 7 days | Unopened only | Not returnable once opened |
| Grocery and gourmet | Not returnable | | Damaged on arrival: report within 48 hours |
| Gift cards | Not returnable | | See the Gift Card Policy |

Electronics includes phones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, headphones, earbuds, chargers, cables and power banks. Opened headphones and earbuds can be returned **only if they are faulty**, for hygiene reasons.

### 2.3 Refunds

Once the returned product is picked up and passes the quality check, the refund goes to your **original payment method**, within **5 to 7 working days of pickup**. You get an SMS and an email when the refund is started.

- UPI, cards and net banking: back to the same account or card.
- EMI: back to the card, and the bank cancels the remaining EMIs (see the Refund Processing Policy).
- Cash on delivery: to a bank account you add in **My Orders** (see the Payments Policy).
- Gift card: back to the ShopKart Gift Card balance.

Delivery fees are refunded only when the whole order is returned because it was damaged, defective or wrong.

### 2.4 Sale and clearance items

Items bought in a sale follow the normal window for their category. They are **not eligible for a price-drop refund** if the price falls later.

Items marked **Clearance** or **Final sale** on the product page cannot be returned for change of mind. If a clearance item has a manufacturing defect, you can ask for an **exchange** within the category's window. A refund is given only when no replacement is available.

### 2.5 Items that cannot be returned

- Products that are used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts
- Innerwear, swimwear, and opened beauty products
- Grocery, unless damaged on arrival
- Custom or personalised products
- Large appliances after installation (report problems to the brand's service centre)
- Software licences and digital codes once revealed

## 3. Quality check

The pickup agent checks the product at your door. If the product does not match the order (for example a different serial number or a missing accessory), the pickup is cancelled and the reason is shared by SMS. You can raise a dispute within 3 days.

## 4. Exceptions

Support agents cannot change these windows. A request outside the window, or for an item listed in section 2.5, must be handed to the support team for review. The team replies within one working day. See the Escalation and Human Handover Policy.
