# Shipping and Delivery Policy

ShopKart delivers to more than 19,000 PIN codes across India. This policy covers delivery charges, delivery times, tracking, address changes and failed deliveries.

## 1. Delivery charges

Delivery charges depend on the order value (after discounts) and the delivery city.

| Order value | Metro cities | Other cities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Below ₹499 | ₹49 | ₹79 |
| ₹499 and above | Free | ₹49 |
| ₹2,999 and above | Free | Free |

**Metro cities** are Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune and Ahmedabad. Every other city, including Kochi, Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore and Chandigarh, counts as "other cities".

Example: an order of ₹799 delivered in Kochi pays ₹49 delivery. The same order in Pune is free.

ShopKart Plus members get free delivery on every order, in every city.

## 2. Delivery times

| Delivery speed | Metro cities | Other cities | Charge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 2–4 days | 4–7 days | As in section 1 |
| Express | Next day | 2–3 days | ₹99 extra |
| Same day | Order by 12 noon | Not available | ₹149 extra |

Times are counted in working days from dispatch. North-East states, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, the Andaman and Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep can take up to 10 working days.

Deliveries happen **between 9 am and 9 pm**, Monday to Saturday. Sunday delivery is available only in metro cities, for Express orders.

## 3. Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number when it is dispatched. Customers can track it in **My Orders** or from the SMS link. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

If tracking has not changed for **3 working days**, or the order is more than **2 days past its promised date**, the customer can raise a "Where is my order" ticket. Support will contact the courier and reply within 24 hours.

## 4. Changing the delivery address

- **Before dispatch:** customers can change the address once, in **My Orders → Change address**, to any serviceable PIN code. The delivery charge is recalculated.
- **After dispatch:** the address cannot be changed online. A support agent can ask the courier to redirect the parcel, but this is not guaranteed and needs a person to approve it. The Copilot must hand these requests to a human.

Changing the phone number for delivery is allowed at any time before the parcel is out for delivery.

## 5. Failed delivery

The courier tries to deliver **three times** on three working days. After three failed attempts, the parcel goes back to our warehouse and the order is cancelled.

- Prepaid orders: full refund including delivery charge, within 5–7 working days after the parcel reaches the warehouse.
- Cash on delivery orders: no charge. If a customer refuses more than **three COD orders in 6 months**, cash on delivery may be turned off for their account.

## 6. Marked as delivered but not received

If tracking says "Delivered" but the customer has not received the parcel, they must report it **within 48 hours** of the delivery time shown. Support checks the courier's proof of delivery (GPS location, photo or OTP). Ask the customer to check with neighbours and building security first.

Claims after 48 hours, and every claim for an order above ₹5,000, go to a human agent.

## 7. Open-box delivery

For phones, laptops and tablets above ₹10,000, the courier opens the box in front of the customer, who checks that the product is correct and not visibly damaged before accepting. The customer can refuse the delivery at the door if the product is wrong or damaged.

## 8. Things we do not deliver

ShopKart does not deliver to PO boxes, or to military areas without a named recipient. Some products (large appliances, furniture) have their own delivery and installation schedule, shown on the product page.
