# Returns Policy (2024)

> ARCHIVED. This policy was replaced on 1 June 2026 by the Returns and Refunds Policy. It is kept for records only. Do not use it to answer customers.

## 1. Return window

All products bought on ShopKart can be returned within **30 days of delivery**, for any reason. This applies to every category, including footwear and electronics.

## 2. Condition

Products should be unused and in their original packaging where possible. Opened electronics are accepted within the 30 days.

## 3. Refunds

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 working days of pickup. Cash on delivery orders are refunded as ShopKart credit.

## 4. Price match

If you find a lower price on another Indian website within 7 days of purchase, ShopKart will refund the difference. See the Price Match Policy.
