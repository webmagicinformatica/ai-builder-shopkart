# Footwear Returns Policy (v1.3)

> ARCHIVED. Replaced on 1 June 2026 by the Footwear Returns and Exchanges Policy (10-day window).

## 1. Return window

Footwear can be returned within **7 days of delivery**, unworn and in the original box.

## 2. Size exchange

One free size exchange is allowed within the same 7 days.

## 3. Clearance

Clearance footwear is final sale.
