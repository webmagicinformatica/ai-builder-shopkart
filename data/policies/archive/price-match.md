# Price Match Policy

> WITHDRAWN on 27 October 2026. ShopKart no longer matches prices. This page was removed from the help centre. It is kept here so the freshness lab can show what happens when a deleted page stays in the index.

## 1. What we match

ShopKart matches the price of an identical product (same brand, model, colour and size) sold by another major Indian online store, if the other store has it in stock.

## 2. How to claim

Send us a link or screenshot of the lower price within 7 days of your purchase. If approved, we refund the difference to your original payment method.

## 3. Exclusions

We do not match prices during sale events, on clearance items, or against marketplace sellers, coupons or bank offers.
