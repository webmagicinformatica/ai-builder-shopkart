# Sale Events, Coupons and Offers Terms

These terms apply to ShopKart sale events, coupons and bank offers. Product-specific offers on a product page take priority over these general terms.

## 1. Sale events in 2026

| Event | Dates | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Republic Day Sale | 20–26 January 2026 | |
| Big Summer Sale | 10–16 May 2026 | |
| Independence Day Sale | 8–15 August 2026 | |
| Big Diwali Sale | 15 October – 2 November 2026 | Coupon DIWALI10 |
| November Clearance | 10–30 November 2026 | Clearance items are final sale |
| Year End Sale | 20–31 December 2026 | Dates may change |

Items bought during a sale event are **sale items**. They follow the normal return window for their category, but are **not eligible for a price-drop refund** (Refund Processing Policy, section 4).

## 2. Coupons

- Only **one coupon per order**.
- A coupon has a minimum order value and a maximum discount, shown with the coupon.
- Coupons can be used with **EMI**, including no-cost EMI, unless the coupon terms say otherwise.
- Coupons cannot be used on gift cards, or with cash on delivery when the coupon says "prepaid only".
- If part of an order is returned or cancelled and the order falls below the coupon's minimum value, the coupon discount is recalculated and the difference is deducted from the refund.

### 2.1 DIWALI10

| Term | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Discount | 10% off |
| Minimum order | ₹1,499 |
| Maximum discount | ₹750 |
| Valid | 15 October – 2 November 2026 |
| Payment | Any method, including EMI and COD |
| Uses | Once per customer |

DIWALI10 cannot be used after 2 November 2026, even for orders placed in the cart earlier.

### 2.2 FIRSTKART

New customers get **₹150 off** their first order of ₹999 or more with coupon FIRSTKART. Prepaid orders only.

## 3. Bank offers

Bank offers give an instant discount when paying with a partner bank's card. Each offer has its own limit, shown at checkout. When an order with a bank offer is returned, the refund is the amount actually paid, after the bank discount.

## 4. Clearance

Products marked **Clearance** are **final sale**: they cannot be returned for change of mind or exchanged for size. If a clearance product is defective or damaged, the customer can ask for an exchange within the category's return window (Returns and Refunds Policy, section 2.4).

## 5. Price match

ShopKart **does not match prices** from other websites or shops. The price match programme ended on 27 October 2026. Earlier promises to match a price are not valid for new orders.

## 6. Quantity limits

During sale events, some products have a limit per customer (for example, 2 per order). Orders above the limit may be cancelled by ShopKart (Order Cancellation Policy, section 5).

## 7. Mistakes in prices

If a price is clearly wrong because of a technical error (for example, a ₹49,999 laptop shown at ₹499), ShopKart may cancel the order and refund the full amount, even after it is confirmed.
