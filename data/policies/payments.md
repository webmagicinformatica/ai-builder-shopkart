# Payments, Cash on Delivery and UPI Policy

This policy lists the payment methods ShopKart accepts, the limits on each, and what to do when a payment fails or is charged twice.

## 1. Accepted payment methods

| Method | Available on | Limits | Extra charge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UPI (any UPI app) | All orders | Up to ₹1,00,000 per order (bank limits apply) | None |
| Debit and credit cards | All orders | As per card limit | None |
| Net banking | All orders | As per bank | None |
| Card EMI | Orders of ₹3,000 and above | Credit cards of partner banks | Bank interest, unless no-cost EMI |
| Cash on delivery (COD) | Orders up to ₹10,000 | Not on gift cards or pre-orders | ₹30 COD fee |
| ShopKart Gift Card | All orders | Can be combined with one other method | None |

ShopKart does **not** accept cheques, demand drafts or cryptocurrency.

## 2. UPI

- Customers pay with any UPI app (or by scanning the QR shown at checkout).
- The payment request expires after **10 minutes**. If the app shows "pending" for longer, do not pay again. Wait 30 minutes; the order is confirmed or the money is reversed automatically.
- Every UPI payment has a 12-digit **UPI reference number** (UTR). Customers need it for any refund or payment ticket.

ShopKart staff will **never** ask for a UPI PIN, an OTP, or ask a customer to "receive money" by entering their PIN. Anyone who does is not from ShopKart.

## 3. Cash on delivery

- COD is available for orders up to **₹10,000**, at serviceable PIN codes.
- A **₹30 COD fee** is added at checkout. It is not refunded if the order is returned, except for damaged, defective or wrong items.
- The courier accepts cash or UPI at the door (scan-to-pay). Exact change is appreciated.
- COD may be turned off for accounts that refuse more than three COD deliveries in 6 months.

### 3.1 Refunds on COD orders

COD orders have no card or UPI account to refund to. When a COD order is returned, the customer adds a bank account (account number and IFSC) in **My Orders → Refund details**. The refund is sent by NEFT within **5–7 working days after the bank details are verified**. Customers may instead choose a ShopKart Gift Card balance, credited within 24 hours.

## 4. EMI

- Card EMI is available on orders of **₹3,000 and above**, with tenures of 3, 6, 9 and 12 months.
- **No-cost EMI** is offered on selected products: ShopKart pays the interest as an instant discount.
- Coupons and sale offers can be used together with EMI unless the offer terms say otherwise.
- Returns on EMI orders are covered in the Refund Processing Policy, section 5.

## 5. Failed and double payments

| Problem | What happens | When to raise a ticket |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money debited, order not placed | Automatic reversal in 5–7 working days | After 7 working days |
| Charged twice for one order | Extra charge reversed in 5–7 working days | After 7 working days |
| UPI shows pending | Resolves within 30 minutes | After 24 hours |
| Card declined | Nothing debited | Not needed; try another method |

For a ticket, the customer should include the order ID and the UPI reference number or the card statement line. Double-charge tickets with proof are reviewed within 2 working days.

## 6. Invoices and GST

Every order gets a GST invoice, available in **My Orders → Invoice** after dispatch. Business customers can add their GSTIN at checkout to claim input tax credit; the GSTIN cannot be added after the invoice is issued.

## 7. Security

Card details are processed by our RBI-compliant payment partner and are never stored on ShopKart servers, except as a tokenised card if the customer chooses to save it. Support agents and the Copilot must never ask for a full card number, CVV, OTP or UPI PIN.
