# Footwear Returns and Exchanges Policy

This policy covers shoes, sandals, slippers, sports shoes and boots. It adds to the Returns and Refunds Policy; where they differ for footwear, this policy applies.

## 1. Return window

Footwear can be returned **within 10 days of delivery**.

> **Change from 1 June 2026:** the footwear window was **7 days** before 1 June 2026. It is now **10 days** for every footwear order delivered on or after 1 June 2026. Answers that say 7 days for footwear, or 30 days for anything, are out of date.

Conditions:

- Unworn outdoors. Trying them on indoors, on a clean floor, is fine.
- In the original shoe box, which must not be damaged or written on. Put the box inside another bag or carton for pickup.
- All tags, extra laces and inserts included.

Socks, insoles sold separately and shoe-care products cannot be returned once opened.

## 2. Size exchanges

Customers can exchange footwear for a different size of the **same product** within the same 10 days.

| Exchange type | Allowed | Charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Different size, same product and colour | Yes, once per order | Free |
| Second size exchange on the same item | Yes | ₹49 pickup fee |
| Different colour, same product | Yes, if in stock | Free, price difference applies |
| Different product | No, return and order again | |

The new pair is sent when the old pair is picked up. If the size is out of stock, the exchange becomes a refund.

## 3. Sale and clearance footwear

- **Sale** footwear follows the normal 10-day window and can be exchanged for size.
- **Clearance** footwear is **final sale**. It cannot be returned or exchanged for size or for change of mind.
- If a clearance pair has a **defect**, the customer can still ask for an **exchange** for the same product within 10 days of delivery. A refund is given only if no replacement pair is available.

Example: the Stride RN220 running shoes in the November clearance are final sale. A customer who simply changed their mind cannot return them; a customer whose pair has a split sole can ask for an exchange.

## 4. What counts as a defect

| Problem | Defect? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sole separating from the upper | Yes | |
| Stitching coming apart within 10 days | Yes | |
| Two different sizes in one box | Yes (wrong item) | See the Damaged, Defective and Wrong Items Policy |
| Sole noise during use (squeaking) that does not stop after the first few days | Yes | Report within 10 days with a short video |
| Colour slightly different from the photo | No | Screens show colours differently |
| Creasing at the toe after wearing | No | Normal for most materials |
| Tight fit | No | Use a size exchange |

A small amount of sole noise in the first one or two days is normal for new rubber soles; see the shoe care guide on the product page.

## 5. Refunds

Refunds for returned footwear go to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of pickup, as in the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 2.3. The delivery charge is refunded only for defective or wrong items.

## 6. Exceptions

A request after 10 days (for example, shoes delivered 12 days ago) is outside the window. The Copilot must say so clearly, and offer to pass the case to the support team, who can review exceptions. It must not promise that an exception will be approved.
