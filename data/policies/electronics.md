# Electronics Returns Policy

This policy covers phones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, fitness bands, headphones, earbuds, speakers, cameras, chargers, cables, power banks and computer accessories. It adds to the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## 1. Return window

Electronics can be returned **within 7 days of delivery**, and **only if they are unopened**, with the manufacturer's seal intact, **unless the item is faulty or damaged**.

- **Unopened, change of mind:** return within 7 days. Full refund of the item price.
- **Opened, working:** not returnable. The customer can use the manufacturer's warranty for any later fault.
- **Faulty or damaged (opened or not):** return or replacement within 7 days of delivery. See the Damaged, Defective and Wrong Items Policy.

After 7 days, faults are handled under the Warranty Policy, not as a return.

## 2. Headphones and earbuds

Opened headphones, earbuds and in-ear monitors can be returned **only if they are faulty**, for hygiene reasons. "Faulty" includes no sound, sound in one side only, charging case not charging, or Bluetooth not pairing after a reset. Fit or comfort is not a fault.

| Smartwatches | 7 days | Not returnable | Replacement or refund | Unpair from phone first |

Laptop bags, phone cases and screen guards (unapplied) are accessories, not electronics, and follow the 14-day window.

## 4. Before a pickup

The customer must:

1. Back up and then **erase all personal data**, and sign out of accounts (Google, Apple ID, Samsung account). A phone locked to an account cannot be accepted.
2. Remove SIM and memory cards.
3. Pack all accessories, manuals, and any free gift that came with the product.

The pickup agent checks the IMEI or serial number against the invoice. If it does not match, the pickup is cancelled.

## 5. Bundles and free gifts

If a product came with a free gift (for example, earbuds with a phone), the free gift must be returned with the main product. If it is missing or used, its listed price is deducted from the refund.

If one item from a bundle is returned (for example, the laptop bag from a laptop + bag combo), only that item's share of the bundle price is refunded.

## 6. High-value electronics

For phones, laptops and tablets above ₹10,000, delivery is **open-box**: the customer checks the product at the door. Damage visible at the door should be refused at delivery.

Any refund above ₹5,000 needs a support lead's approval (Refund Processing Policy, section 1). This covers most phones and laptops.

## 7. Model numbers

Customers often ask about a product by its model number (for example "SonicBuds X2" or "Stride RN220"). Always look up the exact model on the product page; similar model numbers (X2 and X2 Pro, RN210 and RN220) can have different rules, prices and warranties.
