# Refund Processing Policy

This policy explains how ShopKart approves and pays refunds: who can approve them, how long they take for each payment method, and what happens with partial refunds, EMI orders, sale items and double charges. When to accept a return is covered in the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## 1. Approval rules

| Refund amount (per order) | Who approves | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to ₹5,000 | Automatic, if inside policy | Same day |
| Above ₹5,000 | A support lead (human approval) | Within 1 working day |
| Any amount outside policy | A support lead | Within 1 working day |

- **Any refund above ₹5,000 needs a human.** The Copilot and other automated tools can prepare a refund request, but they cannot approve or promise it.
- The total refunded on one order can never be more than the amount paid for that order.
- A customer's **third refund request in 30 days** always goes to a support lead, whatever the amount.
- Automatic refunds across all customers are capped at **₹50,000 per day**. Above the cap, every request waits for a person.

Support agents must never tell a customer that a refund "has been processed" before the system shows it as started. Say that a refund request has been raised, and when they will hear back.

## 2. How long refunds take

Refunds are started after the returned item passes the quality check, or straight away for a cancelled prepaid order.

| Payment method | Refund goes to | Time after refund is started |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UPI | Same UPI account | 5–7 working days |
| Debit or credit card | Same card | 5–7 working days |
| Net banking | Same bank account | 5–7 working days |
| EMI (card) | Same card; EMIs cancelled by bank | 5–7 working days, EMI change on next statement |
| Cash on delivery | Bank account added by customer (NEFT) | 5–7 working days after bank details are verified |
| ShopKart Gift Card | Gift card balance | Within 24 hours |

If a refund has not arrived **within 7 working days** of the SMS that says it was started, the customer should raise a ticket in **Help → Refunds** and include the **UPI reference number** (UTR) or the card's refund reference (ARN) from that SMS. The bank can trace the money with this number.

## 3. Partial refunds

When only some items from an order are returned, only those items are refunded, at the price actually paid for them after discounts.

- If an order-level coupon was used, the discount is shared across items in proportion to their prices, and the refund for each item is reduced by its share.
- If a free gift came with the order and the main item is returned, the free gift must be returned too, or its price is deducted.
- Delivery fees are not refunded on a partial return unless the item was damaged, defective or wrong.

## 4. Price drops

ShopKart refunds the difference if the price of a product drops **within 7 days of delivery**, once per order, when the product is still in the customer's possession.

**Sale items are not eligible for a price-drop refund.** A "sale item" is any product bought during a ShopKart sale event or with a "Deal" or "Sale" badge. Clearance items are also excluded.

## 5. EMI orders

For orders paid with card EMI:

- A **full return** cancels the EMI plan. The bank reverses the principal; any interest or processing fee already charged follows the bank's own rules.
- A **partial return** (for example, one item from a bundle) refunds that item's price as a lump sum to the card. The EMI plan for the rest continues unchanged.
- No-cost EMI discounts given at checkout are deducted from the refund for the returned item.

## 6. Double charges and failed payments

If a customer was charged twice for one order, or charged for an order that failed, the extra amount is reversed automatically within **5–7 working days**. If it has not come back after 7 working days, raise a ticket with the UPI reference number or card statement line. Double charges are never refunded in "24 hours"; do not promise that.

## 7. Refund requests for damaged items

The refunds team replies to every damaged-item request within **48 hours**. A photo of the damage and of the shipping label is required. See the Damaged, Defective and Wrong Items Policy.

## 8. What the Copilot may say

- It may explain the timelines in this policy and raise a refund request.
- It must not approve refunds, promise an amount above ₹5,000, or promise a date earlier than the timelines above.
